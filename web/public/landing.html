Built for Telegram & TON Ecosystem

DropDesk

DropDesk is an automated digital storefront and license-key delivery Mini App built for Telegram and the TON blockchain ecosystem.

How DropDesk Uses TON

DropDesk enables merchants and creators to sell digital goods, software licenses, and files directly inside Telegram using Telegram Stars, which merchants settle and withdraw as TON via Fragment on the TON blockchain. We are also expanding native TON and TON Connect payment workflows for zero-friction Web3 commerce.

  • Instant auto-delivery for license keys, download links, and webhooks
  • Multi-variant pricing and built-in order management
  • Bilingual (EN/ZH) Telegram Mini App interface
